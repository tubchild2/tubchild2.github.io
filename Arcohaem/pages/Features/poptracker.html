Population Simulator

I am but one step closer to automating the tedium of meticulous worldbuilding. Once I combine the functionality of this with the village generator, I can create detailed simulations of entire cities.

In the future, I'd like to add more elements of culture, socialization, and individuality. I want to know who these people are and why they do what they do. I want to know what they do for work, who their friends are, and who they dislike. I want to know how much money they have, what their social class is, if they have any disabilities. I want to know their medical conditions. There's so much I could add to this. I want to know how they'd behave if I implemented taboos (especially against incest cause whoo boy they do it a whole lot). I want to know how good of parents they had / are.
