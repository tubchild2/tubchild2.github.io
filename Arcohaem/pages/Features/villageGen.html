Arcohaem Village Generator

Broadly simulates a village in Arcohaem. Uses out of date history. None of it is wrong, I think, but there's a whole lot more there now. It's the mechanical bones that matter here.

In the future, I'd like to implement the population simulator into this. That handles face-to-face interactions and individuals. This handles structural functionalism; the village manages groups of people to perform tasks. I'd elect a leader, who could make decisions based on the village's AI and on some of his traits. The decisions would revolve around assigning districts, education, politics, resources, construction, and law enforcement.
